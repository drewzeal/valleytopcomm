The Opportunity

Where international businesses come together to create real partnerships.

The Valleytop International Business Conference 2026 is a global platform bringing together business leaders, entrepreneurs, investors, government officials, policymakers, companies, institutions, professionals, students, innovators and international organisations in Helsinki, Finland, from 17–20 November 2026.

The conference creates a unique environment for participants from Africa, Europe and around the world to discover cross-border opportunities from Finland, connect with the Finnish and European ecosystem, establish international relationships and explore opportunities to do business, invest, study, innovate, expand and build long-term partnerships.

Finland is internationally recognised for its strong environment for innovation, education, technology, entrepreneurship, governance and sustainable development. Valleytop International Business Conference provides an opportunity for international participants to gain first-hand insight into opportunities in Finland and Africa while connecting with organisations and decision-makers who can help turn international ambitions into practical opportunities.

Valleytop International Business Conference is designed to be more than a traditional conference. It is an international gateway connecting people and organisations between Finland, Africa and the global market.

For some participants, the journey may begin with a conference ticket and a four-day experience in Helsinki. For others, it may lead to a new company in Finland, European market entry, a new investment, a university admission, a strategic partnership, a government-to-business relationship, a technology collaboration, a new customer, a new career, or an entirely new international future.

We welcome participants from Nigeria, Finland, Europe, Africa, the Americas, Asia, the Middle East and the rest of the world. Whether you represent a government, company, investment organisation, university, startup, established business or institution, or participate as an entrepreneur, student or professional, Valleytop 2026 provides a platform to connect with Finland and explore what comes next.
